Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-27 ｜ App: Cike ｜ Package: com.cike.app

1. Information We Collect

"Cike" is a local-content emotion-text app. We do not require registration, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as name, phone number, email, precise location, contacts, or photos.

To support free operation, the app integrates the Google AdMob advertising SDK and uses Native Ads embedded inline within the "Timeline" feed (marked with an "Ad" label, no pop-ups). When displaying ads, your device's Advertising ID (corresponding to the Android permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) may be used by Google for ad attribution and, if you have not disabled it, personalized advertising. Other than that, we do not collect any other data from you.

2. Advertising and User Consent

  • Ad format: The app uses only Native Ads, embedded in the content feed and marked "Ad", with no pop-ups and no full-screen interstitial ads.
  • User consent (CMP / UMP): In regions such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, in accordance with GDPR and related regulations, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for ad-related personalization and data processing when the app first launches. You may choose whether to allow personalized ads; withholding consent will not affect your use of other app features.
  • United States state privacy laws: In US states with applicable privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, CCPA), we also use Google's UMP to provide a privacy choice, and you may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information used for personalized advertising.
  • You can review and change your consent at any time in-app via "My → Ads & Privacy".
  • If you do not grant consent, we will display only non-personalized ads, or no ads, based on your choice.

3. Storage and Use of Information

  • Your favorites, timeline records, and other user content are stored only in the Room database on your device (cike.db) and are never uploaded to any server.
  • The Advertising ID is processed by the Google AdMob SDK per its privacy policy, for ad display, frequency capping, and attribution.
  • The 800 built-in text entries ship with the app package; reading them requires no internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob / Google Play services: responsible for ad loading, consent (user-messaging) collection, and attribution. See Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); for ad-related data processing and advertising partners, see Google Advertising Policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

5. Your Rights and Controls

  • You can reset the Advertising ID or disable ad personalization in your device system settings (path varies by OS version, usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can manage cross-app ad personalization preferences at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can delete local favorites and timeline records in-app via "My → Clear All Data" (built-in content library is unaffected).
  • We do not collect remote data tied to your account, so there is no remote data export/deletion interface; your local data is entirely under your control.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed with content directed at children, though emotional themes may touch on heartbreak or sadness. We do not knowingly collect personal information of children other than the Advertising ID.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via the in-app "About" page or the developer email.

8. Changes

If this policy is updated, the effective date will be revised both here and within the app.

隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

生效日期：2026-08-27 ｜ 应用：此刻（Cike）｜ 包名：com.cike.app

1. 我们收集的信息

「此刻」是一款以本地内容为主的情绪文字应用。我们不要求注册，也不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、精确位置、通讯录、相册等个人身份信息。

为支持应用的免费运营，本应用集成了 Google AdMob 广告 SDK，使用原生广告（Native Ads）以信息流形式内嵌于「时光轴」中（带「Ad」标识，不弹窗）。在展示广告时，设备的广告标识符（Advertising ID，对应 Android 权限 com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID）可能被 Google 用于广告归因与（在你未停用的前提下）个性化广告投放。除此之外，我们不会收集你的其他数据。

2. 广告与用户同意

  • 广告形式：本应用仅使用原生广告（Native Ads），内嵌于内容信息流，带「Ad」标识，不弹窗、不插入全屏广告。
  • 用户同意（CMP / UMP）：在欧洲经济区（EEA）、英国等适用地区，依据 GDPR 等相关法规，我们通过 Google 的用户消息平台（User Messaging Platform，UMP）在应用首次启动时征求您对广告个性化及相关数据处理的同意。您可自行选择是否允许；不同意不会影响您使用应用的其他功能。
  • 美国各州隐私法：在美国已立法州（如加州 CCPA 等），我们同样通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）提供隐私选择，您可选择退出用于个性化广告的个人信息出售或共享。
  • 您可随时在应用内「我的 → 广告与隐私」重新打开同意表单，查看并修改您的选择。
  • 若您未给予同意，我们将仅展示非个性化广告，或依据您的选择不展示广告。

3. 信息的存储与使用

  • 你的收藏、时光轴记录等用户内容仅保存在你手机本地的 Room 数据库（cike.db），不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 广告标识符由 Google AdMob SDK 按其隐私政策处理，用于广告展示、频次控制与归因。
  • 应用内置的 800 条文字内容随安装包提供，阅读无需联网。

4. 第三方服务

Google AdMob / Google Play 服务：负责广告加载、用户消息（同意）收集与归因。相关数据处理见 Google 隐私政策（https://policies.google.com/privacy）；广告相关的数据处理与广告合作伙伴另见 Google 广告政策（https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads）。

5. 你的权利与控制

  • 你可以在设备系统设置中重置广告 ID 或停用广告个性化（路径因系统版本而异，通常在「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」）。
  • 你可在 Google 广告设置 管理跨应用的广告个性化偏好。
  • 你可以在应用内「我的 → 清空所有数据」删除本地收藏与时光记录（不影响内置内容库）。
  • 我们未收集可关联到你的账号的远程数据，因此无远程数据导出/删除接口；本地数据由你完全掌控。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不含面向儿童的设计内容，但情绪主题可能涉及失恋、难过等。我们不会有意收集儿童的广告 ID 以外的个人信息。

7. 联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，可通过应用内「关于」页或开发者邮箱联系我们。

8. 政策变更

本政策如需更新，将在应用内及本页面同步修订生效日期。
