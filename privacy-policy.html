Privacy Policy Last updated: August 19, 2026 What we collect We collect no personal information. The app does not require registration, login, phone number or email, and it never accesses your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Where your data lives Your favorites and timeline records are stored only in the local database (Room) on your device and are never uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing data removes these records. Network This app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load and display ads, so a network connection is required. Apart from information needed for the ad service to operate, the app does not upload your favorites or timeline records. Sharing Sharing generates a share image on your device and sends it through the system share sheet. It never passes through our servers. Third-party services This app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display native ads on the Timeline page. Google may process device and advertising-related information according to its policies; favorites and timeline records remain stored only on this device. Contact us For privacy-related questions, reach us through the developer contact listed on the app store.
